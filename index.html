      Try a free lesson

      A sample from QUR 101, Introduction to Qur’anic Studies. Read it, then test yourself.

      Lesson 1: What is the Qur’an?

      By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

      • Define the Qur’an in its linguistic and technical meaning
      • Describe how revelation began and how long it lasted
      • Explain how the Qur’an was preserved

      1. The meaning of the word

      Linguistically, Qur’an comes from the Arabic root qara’a, meaning to recite or read. Scholars define it as the speech of Allah revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, transmitted to us by many people in every generation (tawatur), and an act of worship to recite.

      2. How revelation began

      Revelation began in the month of Ramadan while the Prophet ﷺ was in the Cave of Hira near Makkah. The first verses revealed are the opening of Surah al-‘Alaq, which begins with the command to read. Revelation then continued for about twenty-three years, in Makkah and then in Madinah.

      3. How the Qur’an is arranged

      The Qur’an has 114 surahs, beginning with Surah al-Fatihah and ending with Surah an-Nas. It is also divided into 30 equal parts called juz’, which helps with reading and memorising.

      4. How it was preserved

      During the Prophet’s ﷺ lifetime, the Companions memorised the Qur’an and scribes wrote it down. After his death, it was gathered into one volume under Abu Bakr. Later, standard copies were prepared and sent to major cities under ‘Uthman, so that Muslims would recite it in the same way.
